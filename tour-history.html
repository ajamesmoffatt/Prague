Historic tour: Old Town to Malá Strana

A relaxed half-day on foot (roughly 4.5 hours, or 7 with every museum and a proper lunch), mostly outdoors, finishing at the river. It runs along the old Royal Route in reverse: from the Municipal House through the Old Town to Charles Bridge, then across to Malá Strana. Prague Castle is a separate half-day, as its own ticket and a long climb. Click any stop to see it on the map.

Costs below are adult prices (reduced shown where known). Items marked ≈ are approximate and worth checking before you go.

Municipal House (Obecní dům)

10:00 · 40–60 min · Tour 320kč / 270kč reduced

Start at náměstí Republiky. The Art Nouveau mosaic over the door is free to admire; the guided tour takes you through the Smetana Hall and the mayor’s salon, and it’s where Czechoslovak independence was proclaimed in 1918.

Right beside it: Café Paris for a breakfast (the Eggs Royale is the recommended order).

Powder Tower

~11:00 · 10 min · Climb ≈190kč, or free from outside

The Gothic gate at the start of the old Royal Route. You can climb for the view, or just look at the stonework.

House of the Black Madonna

~11:10 · 45 min · 150kč / 80kč students

Czech Cubist design, in a Cubist building (1912). The first-floor Grand Café Orient is the only Cubist café of its kind, if you want an early coffee.

Estates Theatre

~11:55 · 5 min · Free to see from outside; shows from ≈500kč

On Ovocný trh. Mozart conducted the premiere of Don Giovanni here in 1787. Next door is the Carolinum, the old university; the Charles University Bookshop is on the way to the square.

Old Town Square

~12:10 · 60 min · Tower & Town Hall 350kč / 280kč youth; Týn free

The centre of the medieval town, so take it slowly: the Orloj and Old Town Hall (climb the tower for the view), Týn Church, the Jan Hus memorial and Marian Column, Kinsky Palace and St James with its mummified arm. Look down for the 27 white crosses by the Town Hall.

Pass by: Café Mozart and Café Lippert on the square. Pretty, but pay for the view.

Týn Courtyard: Botanicus

~13:10 · 15 min · Gallery 180kč / 80kč

Duck through the passage behind Týn Church into the old Ungelt courtyard for Czech herbal cosmetics and teas. The House at the Golden Ring (city gallery) is a short detour if you want one more museum.

Melantrichova & Havelský Market

~13:30 · 30 min · Free

Walk down Melantrichova past Manufaktura (Czech beer cosmetics, wooden toys), then into the Havelský Market for fruit, honey and nuts. A good snack stop.

Pubs on the way: Uhelný trh to Na Perštýně

~14:00 · lunch option · Mains roughly 200–300kč at the local places

This is the best lunch stretch on the route. U Dvou koček is a cheap local beer hall; U Vejvodů on Jilská is livelier; U Medvídků (brewing since 1466) is a short walk further.

Bethlehem Chapel

~15:00 · 20 min · 60kč

Where Jan Hus preached in Czech from 1402. The hall was rebuilt in the 1950s, but the plain preaching space is moving.

Passing U Zlatého tygra on Husova, where Havel brought Clinton and Albright. It’s pricier now, so one drink is enough.

Klementinum

~15:30 · 1 hr · 380kč / 300kč youth

A guided tour of the Baroque Library and the Astronomical Tower; book the slot in advance in busy months.

Old Town Bridge Tower

~16:40 · 20 min · 250kč / 200kč ages 16–26

Climb for the view down the bridge to the castle; this is the best vantage for the late light.

Charles Bridge

~17:00 · 30 min · Free

Cross slowly: the statues, the bronze plaque of St John of Nepomuk, and the buskers. On the far side, the Lesser Town Bridge Tower is the lower tower and is far less busy.

Lennon Wall & Kampa

~17:40 · 40 min · Free

Follow the Čertovka stream to the Lennon Wall, then onto Kampa Island for the mill wheel and the giant crawling babies. The Franz Kafka Museum and Museum Kampa are right here if you still have energy.

Finish: Lokál U Bílé kuželky

~18:30 · dinner

Classic Czech food and tank Pilsner back on the Malá Strana side of the bridge, at reasonable prices. Alternatives: U Tří Pštrosů (nicer, pricier) or U Hrocha for a beer.

Optional extras

  • Bookshop detour: Knihkupectví Spálená 53 for cheap books and prints (15 min from Bethlehem Chapel).
  • Longer on glass: Moser Crystal on Na Příkopě, next to the Museum of Communism (390kč / 310kč).
  • Mucha and more: Mucha Museum (300kč / 220kč) off Wenceslas Square if you add a second day.

Opening hours and tour slots change by season. Check before you go.
