Historic tour: Old Town to Malá Strana

A relaxed walk in one direction only: from the Municipal House west through the Old Town to Charles Bridge, then across to Malá Strana. It never doubles back. Allow about 5 hours at a steady pace, or around 7 with lunch, the museums and the Klementinum tour. For a true half-day, skip the Klementinum, the House of the Black Madonna and the tower climbs. Prague Castle is a separate half-day, with its own ticket and a long climb. Click any stop to see it on the map.

Costs below are adult prices (reduced shown where known). Items marked ≈ are approximate and worth checking before you go.

Dotted line = order of stops (straight lines, not street routes). Tap a pin for details.

1Municipal House (Obecní dům)

10:00 · 40 min · Tour 320kč / 270kč reduced

Start at náměstí Republiky. The Art Nouveau mosaic over the door is free to admire. The guided tour takes you through the Smetana Hall and the mayor’s salon, and it’s where Czechoslovak independence was proclaimed in 1918.

Right beside it: Café Paris for breakfast.

2Powder Tower

~10:45 · 10 min · Climb ≈190kč, or free from outside

The Gothic gate at the start of the old Royal Route. Climb for the view, or just admire the stonework from outside.

3House of the Black Madonna

~10:55 · 30 min · 150kč / 80kč students

Czech Cubist design inside a Cubist building (1912). The first-floor Grand Café Orient is a Cubist café, if you want an early coffee.

4Estates Theatre

~11:30 · 5 min · Free to see from outside; shows from ≈500kč

Mozart conducted the premiere of Don Giovanni here in 1787. Next door is the Carolinum, the old university.

5Charles University Bookshop (Celetná)

~11:35 · 10 min · Free

A good stop for Czech-history books and university gifts, on the way down Celetná towards the square.

6Týn Courtyard: Botanicus

~11:50 · 15 min · Gallery 180kč / 80kč

Duck through the passage into the old Ungelt courtyard, behind Týn Church, for Czech herbal cosmetics and teas. The House at the Golden Ring (city gallery) is next door if you want one more museum.

7Old Town Square

~12:10 · 50 min · Tower & Town Hall 350kč / 280kč youth; Týn free

The centre of the medieval town, so take it slowly: the Orloj and Old Town Hall (climb the tower for the view), Týn Church, the Jan Hus memorial and Marian Column, Kinsky Palace and St James with its mummified arm. Look down for the 27 white crosses by the Town Hall.

Pass by: Café Mozart and Café Lippert on the square. Pretty, but you pay for the view.

8Havelský Market (Havelská)

~13:05 · 20 min · Free

Head south down Melantrichova to the open-air stalls for fruit, honey and nuts. Touristy, but a classic stop, and a good snack.

9Manufaktura (Melantrichova)

~13:25 · 10 min · Free

Czech-made gifts: beer cosmetics, wooden toys and kitchenware. Good for presents.

10Lunch: U Dvou koček

~13:35 · 60 min · Mains roughly 200–300kč

A cheaper, local-feeling beer hall in the middle of the tourist zone. Alternatives within a few minutes: U Vejvodů on Jilská (livelier) and U Medvídků (brewing since 1466).

11Bethlehem Chapel

~14:40 · 20 min · 60kč

Where Jan Hus preached in Czech from 1402. The hall was rebuilt in the 1950s, but the plain preaching space is moving.

On the way: U Zlatého tygra on Husova, where Havel brought Clinton and Albright. It’s pricier now, so one drink is plenty.

12Klementinum

~15:05 · 60 min · 380kč / 300kč youth

A guided tour of the Baroque Library and the Astronomical Tower. Book the slot in advance in busy months. Skip it if you want a shorter day.

13Old Town Bridge Tower

~16:10 · 20 min · 250kč / 200kč ages 16–26

Climb for the view down the bridge to the castle; this is the best vantage for the late light.

14Charles Bridge

~16:30 · 30 min · Free

Cross slowly: the statues, the bronze plaque of St John of Nepomuk, and the buskers.

15Lesser Town Bridge Tower

~17:00 · 15 min · ≈190kč; both towers together ≈340kč

The lower tower at the far end. Far less busy than the Old Town one, and a good last view back across the river. Optional climb.

16Finish: Lennon Wall & Kampa Island

~17:15 · 45 min · Free

Follow the Čertovka stream to the Lennon Wall, then onto Kampa Island for the mill wheel and the giant crawling babies. The Franz Kafka Museum and Museum Kampa are right here if you still have energy. For dinner, Lokál U Bílé kuželky (classic Czech, tank Pilsner), U Tří Pštrosů (nicer, pricier) or U Hrocha (a beer).

Optional extras

  • Bookshop detour: Knihkupectví Spálená 53 for cheap books and prints, if you’re happy to add a 15-minute walk off the route.
  • Longer on glass: Moser Crystal on Na Příkopě, next to the Museum of Communism (390kč / 310kč).
  • Mucha and more: Mucha Museum (300kč / 220kč) off Wenceslas Square if you add a second day.

Opening hours and tour slots change by season. Check before you go.
