Pub crawl: Old Town to New Town (pub golf)

Eight stops, about 4–5 hours, walking between them (all under ten minutes). Each stop has a par: the number of sips to finish your drink. Count your sips, add them up, and the lowest score wins. It’s deliberately weighted towards local beer and Czech spirits. Click any stop to see it on the map. Prices are rough guides: Czech beer is cheap, central cocktails are not.

Short version (five holes): stops 1, 3, 5, 7 and 8. The strong beer at stop 5 is a half-way slowdown, not a challenge. Eat before you start, and drink water between stops.

Finish

Pick one: Chapeau Rouge (student basement, close by); Karlovy Lázně (huge and touristy, five floors); Reduta Jazz Club (live jazz, intimate); or Lucerna Music Bar (live bands). Check entry and cover on the night.

Order like a local

  • Hladinka is the standard pour with a creamy head. Šnyt is a shorter pour with more foam. Mlíko (“milk”) is almost all foam, sweet, and an acquired taste.
  • 10° vs 12°: the lighter beer or the heavier one (see the Tips page).
  • Phrases: Na zdraví (cheers), Dvě piva, prosím (two beers, please), Zaplatíme, prosím (the bill, please).

Practical

  • Cash: Popocafepetl is cash only; take some, from an accredited bank, not Euronet.
  • Getting home: metro stops around midnight; night trams and buses run after that.
  • Pace yourself: this is a lot of drinks. Skip stops, share the strong beer, and never leave your drink unattended.
  • Other crawl options: the Drunken Monkey Bar Crawl is promoted by Hostel Bunker Factory (English speakers, good for meeting people).

Prices are rough guides and change. Opening hours too: check before you go.
