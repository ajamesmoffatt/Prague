The Coast of Bohemia

A guide to the seaside town of Prague by James Moffatt

MapTips & food to try

Tips

  • Skip the tourist circle for food. Old Town is where ALL the tourists go: decent food at trumped-up prices. Eat nearer Náměstí Míru, Hradčany, Malá Strana or Smíchov for better food at better prices.
  • Cash. Don’t use Euronet ATMs, especially in Old Town. ČSOB, Raiffeisen or KB are accredited banks and charge about a £5 fee. Cards are taken in most places, but get some emergency cash.
  • Old Town museums are touristy gimmicks. Skip them for something proper, or go for a laugh at the bizarre exhibits.
  • Walk everywhere. You’ll find cutesy side roads and churches tourists never see. Smíchov to Masaryk Station is only about 40 minutes.
  • Transport. A 3-day metro/tram ticket is 330kč. Don’t risk travelling without one; the ticket inspectors are scary.
  • Vintage photo booths (100kč / £4): Vodičkova (Dům U Nováků) and the Karlín Barracks.
  • Keep an eye out for the statues and works of art that most people never notice.
  • Christmas: markets at Wenceslas Square, Old Town Square and Náměstí Míru (more for locals). Czechs eat carp, and you’ll see live fish in the centre; they keep them in the bath until Christmas Eve.
  • Also heard good things: Hemingway Bar (rum and absinthe), Standard Café (beer and cocktails, popular with journalism students), Wine Not on Masarykovo nábřeží, Café Mozart (touristy, pricey food) and Café Lippert on Old Town Square.

Food & drink to try

Guláš – meat and dumplings in a spicy sauce
Svíčková – meat and dumplings in a creamy sauce
Smažený sýr – fried cheese, usually with chips
Chlebíčky – little open sandwiches, cheap and actually eaten by Czechs
Kachna – roast duck with dumplings and red cabbage
Řízek – schnitzel
Beef tartare – raw meat with an egg
Koleno – roast pig knee with crackling; a lot of food for your money
Koláče – sweet pastry rolls: plum jam, cheese or poppy seed
Trdelník – sweet street pastry. NOT traditional Czech
Kofola – “communist cola”, a bit liquorice-y. Almdudler – Austrian almondy drink that settles the stomach
Drinks – Becherovka, absinthe, slivovice, any beer (especially Kozel; Kozel Dark is delicious). 10° is the lightest beer, 12° the heavier
Vegetarians – Czechs count anything under 15% meat as veggie. The centre has vegan restaurants, but it isn’t the same as proper Czech food.

Prices are from memory. Double-check opening hours before you go.
